I am an 18 year old high school (almost university) student living in Germany. I moved to Germany from Australia in 2018, yet my parents are Persian and I was born in Dubai. I'm passionate about mathematics, physics, biology, chemistry and philosophy, even though I larp most of it! I'm in the middle of my first existential crisis and I hope it will be over soon.

I am making this blog to preserve my thoughts in a place where they're safer than on paper. I hope that I can one day laugh about how stressed out I am right now, and that my future self can look back and see how good his life is.
